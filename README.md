# Noor & Machine, in public

The tools and experiments from [noorandmachine.com](https://noorandmachine.com) that anyone can use.

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Test with AI — v1](test-with-ai/v1/) | A crowd of isolated AI agents that choose between options against your brief, many times over. Runs on your machine with your own Claude Code login. [Read the post](https://noorandmachine.com/design/test-with-ai/). |
| [Grade 2 Orange](grade-2-orange/) | A French-greetings practice app for a grade-2 child: video lessons with quick checks, spelling rules, practice, star rounds and a sticker book. Works offline on a tablet. [Try it](https://shaheryarwarraich.github.io/grade-2-orange/). |
